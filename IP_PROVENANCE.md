## Original product direction recorded before any future university enrollment

The project was conceived and directed by Yuxuan Huang before any future enrollment at Columbia University. Its documented product direction includes:

1. A single-wish experience that preserves a compact, user-controlled profile rather than treating every conversation as unrelated.
2. Original coach configurations that translate broad schools of manifestation thought into distinct product methods without impersonating living creators.
3. A bilingual Chinese and English experience with responsive desktop navigation and a mobile layout intended for an iOS release.
4. A daily audio-practice studio with user-written affirmations, voice recording, uploaded audio, layered ambient sound, timers, and check-ins.
5. A vision-board studio divided between licensed-image discovery and user-provided photographs, with collage editing rather than AI-generated source photography as the default direction.
6. Story, revision, conversation-memory, wish-archive, and new-wish flows organized around continuity of one chosen goal.
7. An original living-dough brand concept: flour and water becoming responsive dough, fermentation representing an intention already set in motion, stretching and returning as an opening-motion language, and a soft "wish fold" as a recognizable feature rather than a wound-like mark.
8. A tactile and sensory brand direction drawing on softness, kneading, expansion, ASMR, whispering, and intentionally imperfect charm.

Private details of any individual user's wish are intentionally excluded from this provenance record.
